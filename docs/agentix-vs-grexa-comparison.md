# AGENTiX vs Grexa.ai — Full Feature Comparison

> **Purpose:** a complete, side-by-side comparison of what **AGENTiX** provides vs what **Grexa.ai** provides.
> **Date:** 2026-09-17 · reflects the current shipped state of AGENTiX (Unified Leads, Google Business Profile, Google Ads, and Cross-channel ROI are all built).
> Legend: ✅ = provided · ⚠️ = partial/basic · ❌ = not provided

---

## 1. One-line summary

- **Grexa.ai** = a simple, single-business app that helps a local shop **get found on Google (Google Business Profile / local SEO)** and do **basic WhatsApp chat + WhatsApp marketing**, on one flat price.
- **AGENTiX** = a deep, **multi-tenant (agency-ready) growth platform**: WhatsApp **+ Instagram** engagement, a real **CRM + campaigns + automation + AI**, **Google Business Profile**, **Google Ads**, a **Unified Lead Hub**, and **cross-channel ROI** — everything a business does to acquire, engage, convert and measure, in one place.

**Bottom line:** AGENTiX is a **superset of Grexa** — we do everything Grexa does (and deeper), plus Google Ads, unified multi-channel attribution, cross-channel ROI, Instagram, a full CRM/campaign engine, and agency multi-tenancy. Grexa still leads on three things: **native mobile apps**, a **"Free GBP Report" lead-magnet funnel**, and **ultra-simple SMB packaging**.

## 3. What AGENTiX provides

### Engagement & Inbox
- **Unified real-time inbox** — WhatsApp **and Instagram/Messenger**, with assignment, statuses, labels, spam/sentiment, snooze, internal notes, campaign-scoped chat view, media & templates.
- **AI auto-reply bot** — per-business persona, **Knowledge-Base RAG grounding**, multilingual (English/Hindi/Hinglish/Marathi/Tamil/Telugu/Kannada/Bengali), interactive buttons, AI-provider failover, and **business-only safety guardrails**.
- Bot pause / human handoff, auto-CSAT, auto-resolve, auto-tagging.

### CRM & Leads
- **Contacts + Contact 360** — full profile, conversations, orders, CSAT, lifetime value, tags, temperature, VIP, lifecycle, CSV/bulk import, custom fields, **cross-channel journey timeline**.
- **CRM Pipeline** — stages, Kanban, **AI lead scoring**, temperature auto-cooldown, assignment, stage history.
- **Unified Lead Hub** — **every lead from every channel** (WhatsApp, Instagram, Meta Ads, Google Ads, Google Business, website, chat widget, campaign, API, manual) in one place, each tagged with its **source/channel + first/last touch**, with a **source-breakdown chart** and filters.

### Growth & Acquisition
- **Google Business Profile** — connect the business's GBP; manage **reviews (AI-drafted replies → human approve → post)**, **posts (offers/updates/events)**, **Q&A**, and **performance insights** (views, calls, directions), all from AGENTiX.
- **Google Ads** — **① Lead capture:** Google Ads Lead-Form submissions flow straight into the Unified Hub (no setup barrier). **② Reporting:** connect the Ads account to see campaigns, spend, clicks, conversions.
- **Meta Lead Ads** — Facebook/Instagram lead-ad capture into conversations + KPIs + export.
- **Website chat widget** + **catalog**.

### Campaigns & Automation
- **Campaigns** — broadcast, audience filters, scheduling, **A/B testing**, **carousel**, **limited-time offers**, media headers, per-recipient reply attribution, daily stats, exports.
- **Templates** — full WhatsApp template management + Meta submission + header media + **native WhatsApp Flows / in-chat forms**.
- **Automation** — inbox rules, time-triggers, follow-up sequences, abandoned-cart + Shopify, birthday/re-engagement triggers, auto-assign.
- **AI Revenue** — lead scoring, revenue insights, best-send-hour, hot-lead detection.
- **Knowledge Base** — vector (RAG) documents powering the AI.

### Measurement
- **Analytics** — cross-period KPIs, message funnel, delivery/read/reply rates, CSAT, conversations, leads, campaigns, agents (accurate & uncapped).
- **Cross-channel Marketing ROI** — **spend + leads + conversions + cost-per-lead + conversion rate per channel, in one view** (Google Ads spend + WhatsApp messaging cost joined with leads by channel).

### Platform / Commercial
- **Multi-tenant, agency-ready** — a super-admin manages many client workspaces; role-based access (admin/manager/agent); per-workspace WhatsApp/Google credentials; RLS-isolated data; onboarding + guided setup; security-hardened.
- **Billing** — Razorpay subscriptions/checkout/invoices/plans + Meta ad-spend tracking.
- **Public REST API**, Bookings & Events, Tasks, Support tickets, Reports/Exports.

> **Note on Google features:** the GBP and Google Ads **software is fully built and live**. GBP live data begins once Google approves the GBP API access request (submitted; ~7–10 days), and Google Ads reporting shows numbers once the connected account runs campaigns. Google Ads **lead capture** works immediately.
