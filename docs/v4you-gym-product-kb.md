## Before you paste — four things only you can decide

The bot will repeat whatever is written here, to everybody, so these are settled first.

### 1. The product needs a name

`Max Register` is the gym's own brand, built for one client. It cannot be what V4you
sells. Every document below says **`[PRODUCT NAME]`** — replace it everywhere before
pasting. Some options, all free of any client's brand:

- **GymDesk** — plain, says what it is
- **FitRegister** — closest to what it actually does
- **V4you Gym** — leans on V4you's own name, nothing new to build

### 2. What ₹1,999/month includes, and what it does not

WhatsApp is not free. Meta charges per conversation, and a gym messaging 300 members about
renewals every month will run up a real bill. Decide now:

- Is the Meta/WhatsApp message cost **inside** ₹1,999, or billed on top?
- Is there a **setup fee** on the monthly plan, or is ₹1,999 the whole first payment?
- Is there a **minimum term**, or can they stop any month?

Until you tell me, the KB says a person from V4you will confirm message costs. That is the
safe answer — quoting "all included" and then billing extra is how a client is lost.

### 3. What ₹30,000 one-time actually buys

"Lifetime" needs a boundary or it becomes an argument later:

- Hosting and the domain — included, or the gym's own cost?
- **Updates** — do they get new features built later, or the version shipped on the day?
- **Support** after handover — for how long, and then what?
- Who holds the data if V4you stops operating it?

### 4. The attendance kiosk needs hardware

Face attendance runs on an Android phone at reception, on a stand, with a light. Decide
whether the gym buys it, V4you supplies it, or it is an optional extra — and roughly what
it costs. The KB currently says the gym provides an Android phone and V4you helps choose
it.

### Writing templates that Meta approves

- **No ALL CAPS, no "ACT NOW", no "limited offer"** on a first-contact template. Those are
  what gets a Marketing template rejected.
- **A variable cannot start or end the message**, and two cannot sit next to each other.
- **Utility vs Marketing matters**: a Utility template (like the demo follow-up, which
  continues a conversation the owner started) is cheaper and approves more easily. Do not
  mark a cold pitch as Utility — it gets the whole number flagged.
- Put the price in the template. A gym owner who has to ask is one who does not reply.
